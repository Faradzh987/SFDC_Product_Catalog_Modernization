Exclusions — Exhibit A, Section IV.A

No abatement is owed if: (1) Customer is in breach or default at the time of the failure. (2) The failure results from customer equipment, applications, acts or omissions of customer or customer parties. (3) The failure results from a Force Majeure Event. The SLA abatement is also the sole and exclusive remedy — customer waives all other claims.

What This Calculator Does NOT Do

❌ Does not pull Monthly License Fees from the MSA — fees come from your Order document or Salesforce

❌ Does not apply flat credit percentages — all credits are pro-rata abatements

❌ Does not handle multiple simultaneous failures from different events — each incident needs separate calculation

❌ Does not calculate credits for Environmental Stability (Article II) or Connectivity (Article III) failures

❌ Does not verify whether customer was in default — user must confirm from billing records
